Game Description:

The player is playing Minecraft in single-player, normal survival mode.

Some useful Minecraft cues to look for in screenshots include, but are not limiteed to:
- Health/armor/hunger bars, status effects, XP level.
- Inventory/hotbar contents (tools, weapons, blocks, food).
- Environment: biome, time of day, caves, villages, structures.
- Threats: mobs, lava, fall risk, drowning, nightfall.
- Progress: crafting upgrades, mining finds, building, exploration.
